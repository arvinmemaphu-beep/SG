About Us

Welcome to Soonest Global

A registered domestic corporation with a global reach — accredited and licensed for logistics business for local and international clients, both Commercial and Personal Effect (Balikbayan) shipments.

SOONEST Global Express Corporation is a Filipino-owned company engaged in total complete logistics both in International & Domestic via Sea, Air and Land. Reaching twenty-five (25) years in the industry is one of our distinguishing merits. Consisting of 125 highly competent and seasoned professionals, we preserve the essence of swift, efficient and innovative forwarding.

Our people strictly adhere to the highest degree of professional work ethics and have earned the respect of the government, our clients, and industry peers.

Our Mission

We are the only option in rendering fast, safe and reliable cargo management nationwide through our Road Express Delivery service and a globally diversified logistics source.

Our Vision

To be the preferred global logistics provider and the leading Filipino forwarding company offering Road Express Delivery nationwide.
